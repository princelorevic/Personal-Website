01 — About Me

Building systems, not just websites.

I didn't start my journey as a web developer. I found my way into development through programming, IT support, system testing, and real-world operations.

While studying, I was already building programs using C++ and Visual Basic. These gave me my first foundation in programming, logic, and problem-solving.

After completing my studies, I entered the IT field and worked as a Desktop Support Engineer. My responsibilities gradually expanded beyond technical support into system troubleshooting, software testing, quality assurance, homecare support, and business operations.

I also took on responsibilities as a Delivery Supervisor, where I experienced the operational side of the business and worked with both people and systems. These roles gave me a different perspective on technology — not just how systems are built, but how they are actually used to solve problems in a real working environment.

Throughout those experiences, I slowly realized that building systems was the direction I genuinely wanted to pursue. The more I worked with technology and business processes, the more I wanted to be the person creating the solutions rather than only supporting them.

That realization became even clearer after a conversation with the COO of the company. After learning about the different responsibilities I had taken on, we discussed where I genuinely saw myself in the future. That conversation helped me recognize that software development was the path I wanted to pursue.

Today, I am focused on becoming a full-stack developer, building practical and database-driven applications using JavaScript, Node.js, Express.js, MySQL, HTML, and CSS.
